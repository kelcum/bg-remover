Background Remover

Drop an image or GIF in. It comes out with the background gone, saved as a transparent GIF (or PNG). Everything runs on your machine — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

GIF transparency is all-or-nothing per pixel (no soft edges). Lower this if too much of the subject is disappearing; raise it if background halos remain.

Background removal runs a neural network fully in your browser (via WebAssembly/ONNX). On first use it downloads model files (roughly 45–90 MB depending on quality) which are then cached by the browser. No image data ever leaves your device.
